# ShaktiCare AI

ShaktiCare AI is an AI-powered women’s health platform designed for early PCOD risk prediction using a personalized Digital Twin approach. The platform analyzes menstrual cycle data, symptoms, mood, and lifestyle patterns using Machine Learning models to provide personalized risk insights, preventive recommendations, health analytics, awareness resources, and anonymous support features.

---

# **Features**

* Early PCOD risk prediction using Machine Learning
* Personalized Digital Twin health modeling
* Menstrual cycle, symptom, mood, and lifestyle tracking
* Interactive dashboard with health analytics
* Personalized preventive recommendations
* Anonymous support system for safe interaction
* Health awareness and educational resources

---

## **Backend**

* Decision Tree

## **Database & Tools**

* Python
* NumPy

---

# **Project Objective**

To provide an intelligent and preventive healthcare platform that helps women monitor health patterns, identify early PCOD risks, and make informed health decisions through AI-driven insights.
